# antislop audit 002, 22 November 2026

Mode: **after** (session override). Scope: the popup, the side panel and the archive page (Overview, Conversations, a conversation, Export, Settings) at version 1.7.0, commit `f7c1884`. Direction: [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md), dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

The focus is the one you asked for:
- a simpler popup;
- the archive page's layout and navigation;
- the export interface;
- consistent spacing, type and widths;
- less visual complexity, with every function kept.

You also asked for dark and light mode: see [Dark and light mode](#dark-and-light-mode).

Nothing has been changed. Pick the numbers to fix; numbers not picked stay as they are.

Priority follows the rule's tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks and craftsmanship = LOW. The Liveliness checks (focal point, one accent) have no tier and are rated MEDIUM, as in audit 001. Finding 11 is a Quality Lock, so it would be LOW, but it is listed as MEDIUM because you asked for it.

None of the proposals touches the extraction engine, the selectors, the database, or the export and import code. Every fix changes markup, CSS or the interface scripts in `src/ui/`. Where a proposal moves a control, the function behind it stays.

## How it was checked

- **Pages and data.** The pages were built from the current source and opened in a browser, with stubs for the extension APIs. The archive was seeded as in audit 001: a 451-message group chat *Design review* with a picture and reactions, and a 1:1 chat *Alice Andersen*.
- **Widths:**
  - 1280 and 900 px with a mouse;
  - 375 and 320 px with a touch screen emulated;
  - the popup at its 380 px.
- **Themes:** light and dark.
- **Popup states:**
  - idle on a chat;
  - **More** open;
  - a run in progress;
  - a stopped run.
- **Measurements.** Sizes and positions were read from the rendered page (`getBoundingClientRect`, `getComputedStyle`).
- **Contrast** was not measured again. No colour changed since audit 001, and `tests/unit/logo.test.ts` keeps every text pair at 4.5:1 or more and the control outlines at 3:1 or more, in both themes.
- **Audit 001.** Its findings are fixed and not repeated here.

## Findings

### HIGH

**1. R-03: the side panel scrolls sideways when it is narrower than 363 px.**
At 320 px the page is 363 px wide.
- The capture select is 326 px wide. Its longest option, "New messages since the last update (3 days ago)", sets its minimum width, and the popup's cards cannot shrink below it.
- The chat line, the hint and the option text are cut off at the right.

Where: `src/ui/ui.css` (`.popup-main`, `#view-main`, `.capture-row select`), `src/ui/popup.ts:361`.

Fix:
- `min-width: 0` on the popup's grid items and `max-width: 100%` on selects;
- the option becomes "New messages since the last update" (the hint already says when that was).

**2. R-03: the links that open a conversation are too small to tap.**
Measured with touch emulated at 375 px:

| Link | Height |
| --- | --- |
| The conversation name in the list | 19 px |
| **All conversations**, above a chat | 16 px |
| The names under Latest activity | 37 px |

In the list, the name is the only way to open a chat.

Fix: on a touch screen these links become blocks at least 44 px tall (32 px with a mouse), as audit 001 did for buttons.
Where: `a.conv-title`, `#conv-view-back` and `.activity a` in ui.css.

**3. R-03 (hover-only, antislop-layoutmobile) and R-39: some information exists only in tooltips.**
A touch screen has no hover, so these are never shown:
- what the presets **Knowledge system** and **Full backup** include;
- why **File attachments** and **Reply context** are greyed out for JSON and the package ("Always kept in archive records");
- what **Visible only** does;
- the exact time of a chat's last update in the list;
- which formats **Export…** offers.

Fix: each is written on the page; see 9 for Export and 6 for Visible only. The tooltips can stay as a second copy.
Where: `archive.html:77-78, 150-151`, `export-panel.ts:219`, `popup.html:71`, `archive/conversations.ts:91`.

**4. R-03 (two-state layout, antislop-layoutmobile): the tables hold up only at their two extremes.**
- **Conversations at 900 px:** the four buttons of each row take about 260 px. As a result:
  - names wrap;
  - the key breaks mid-word ("title:alice a ndersen");
  - the archived period takes three lines.
- **Conversations below 760 px:** each conversation becomes a card of 13 labelled lines, with its four buttons in a 2 × 2 block. Two chats make a page 1,005 px tall.
- **Recent runs below 760 px:** each run is a card of ten labelled lines, 257 px tall, and the table keeps up to 50 runs.

Fix, for Conversations:
- The rows lose their buttons. Export…, Rename, Merge… and Delete are already on each conversation's page, one click away.
- The list keeps the picture count link.
- The key leaves the list. It stays on the conversation's page, and search still finds it.
- *History* and *Archived period* become one column.
- Below 760 px a designed card replaces the labelled lines:
  - the name, as a 44 px link;
  - "Group chat · 451 messages · 1 picture";
  - one line for the history and the last update.

Fix, for Recent runs:
- one *Result* column ("450 new · 0 updated · 0 already archived");
- a three-line card below 760 px;
- the five latest runs, then **Show all runs (N)** (see 7).

Where: `src/ui/archive/conversations.ts`, `src/ui/archive/overview.ts`, `archive.html:50-66, 95-109`, and the `table.responsive` rules in ui.css.

### MEDIUM

**5. Liveliness, focal point: during a run, Stop is below the popup's fold.**
DESIGN.md makes the progress and **Stop** the popup's focal point during a run, and an Edge popup is at most 600 px tall.
- With **More** closed, Stop is at 619 to 651 px.
- With **More** open, it is at 719 to 751 px.
- Above it, the capture card stays on screen, disabled.

Fix: hide the capture card while a run is in progress, and name the run in the run card ("New messages since the last update · 12 s"). Progress and Stop then come right after the chat card.
Where: `src/ui/popup.ts` (`renderStatus`), `popup.html:51-102`.

**6. Liveliness, focal point: the idle popup has more parts than its job.**
Its job is to capture this chat. Today it shows:
- a chat card;
- a Capture card with a select and a three-line hint;
- a 51 px wide **Start**;
- **More**, which holds:
  - a second capture button, **Visible only**;
  - the re-read option;
  - the count of messages on screen;
  - a Troubleshooting link.

Fix:
- **Visible only** becomes an option of the Capture select, *Only the messages on screen now*, with its own hint, and the separate button goes.
- "12 messages on screen" moves to the chat card, next to what is archived.
- **More** becomes **Options**: the re-read option and the Troubleshooting link.
- **Start** becomes a full-width primary button that says what it captures (see 13).

The popup then has one select, one button and one disclosure, and keeps every function.

**7. Liveliness, focal point: Overview leads with five equal boxes, not with whether the archive is safe.**
- The backup line is 12 px text under the totals.
- At 375 px the totals take three rows (2 + 2 + 1), and **Back up now…** starts 398 px down the page.
- *Storage used* repeats the header line and Settings → Storage.

Fix:
- a Backup card comes first: the status at 14 px and **Back up now…**;
- then one row of four totals, without Storage used;
- Recent runs shows the five latest, then **Show all runs (N)**.

Where: `archive.html:28-70`, `archive/overview.ts`.

**8. Liveliness, focal point: a conversation opens 13,000 px away from its newest message.**
The view loads the newest 200 messages and shows them oldest first, so it opens on messages from nine days before the newest. At 1280 px the page is 13,303 px tall, and the newest message is at its end.

Fix:
- the view opens scrolled to the newest message;
- **Show earlier messages** stays at the top;
- a **Back to the top** link follows the last message;
- the chat's heading still receives the focus, without scrolling, so keyboard and screen reader users start where they do now.

Where: `archive/conversation-view.ts`, `archive.ts:126-129`.

**9. Liveliness, focal point: Export asks for the details before the main choice.**
- The form starts with the conversation list, and the format comes second.
- The sentence that says what a format produces sits under the Content box, far from the format select.
- The presets sit top right, described only in tooltips (finding 3).
- For PDF, JSON and the package, Packaging is a greyed-out select with one option.

Fix, in this order:
1. **Start from**: Knowledge system or Full backup, each with its one-line description visible.
2. **Format**, with its explanation right under it.
3. **Packaging**, only when there are two choices; otherwise the explanation says how the file is packaged.
4. **Conversations**, **Messages** and **Content**, with a visible note under greyed-out options: "Always included in JSON and package exports."
5. The **Export** button (finding 13).

Where: `archive.html:144-193`, `export-panel.ts`.

**10. R-06: section headings are the size of body text.**
These headings inside cards are 14 px, the body size; only their weight sets them apart:
- Latest activity, Recent runs;
- Messages;
- Import;
- General, Storage, Troubleshooting, About.

DESIGN.md gives section titles 16 and 20 px.

Fix: `h3` at 16 px.

**11. R-21 and your request: the theme switch is hard to reach.**
See [Dark and light mode](#dark-and-light-mode) for what exists.
- The **Theme** field sits in the Settings form. Its other fields wait for **Save settings**, but Theme applies at once: two ways of saving in one form.
- The popup and side panel cannot change the theme.

Fix:
- **Archive page:** a labelled **Theme** select at the right of the header, in place of the storage line. Overview and Settings → Storage still show the storage.
- **Popup and side panel:** a third header button that steps through System, Light and Dark. It has an icon for each, and its name and tooltip give the current choice ("Theme: Dark").
- **Settings:** the Theme field leaves the form.

The theme icons are drawn like the other three (1.5 px stroke) and added to DESIGN.md's list of icons.

### LOW

- Each of the 200 messages on a page has its own border and background, inside the bordered Messages card.

**14. R-05, C-1: bordered boxes inside the Export card.**

**16. R-20: "partial" means two things.**

Fix: one function gives the wording everywhere:
- *Complete*;
- *Not back to the start*;
- *Not checked*, for the third case.

## Dark and light mode

Both exist and work:
- **Settings → General → Theme** offers *System (follows Windows)*, *Light* and *Dark*.
- The choice applies at once to the popup, the side panel and the archive page, before anything is drawn, so no page flashes in the other theme.
- Both themes were checked again here, on the popup and on every archive view.
- The palette for each is in DESIGN.md, and `tests/unit/logo.test.ts` keeps both themes' text and control contrast.

What is missing is a quick way to switch: finding 11 puts a **Theme** select in the archive page's header and a theme button in the popup and side panel.

If you meant something else, for example a second dark palette, or dark as the default for everyone, say which. Today the default is *System*, as R-21 prefers when nothing argues for a fixed theme.
